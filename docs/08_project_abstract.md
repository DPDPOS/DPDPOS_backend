## Legal basis used for these documents

This product documentation is grounded in the official DPDP Act, 2023, the DPDP Rules, 2025, and the 2025 notification establishing the Data Protection Board of India. The Act governs digital personal data processing, the Rules provide operational detail and staged commencement, and the Board notification establishes the enforcement body.  

The product documents below are intentionally structured around:
- Data Fiduciary / Data Principal terminology,
- consent and notice workflows,
- rights handling,
- security safeguards,
- breach handling,
- significant data fiduciary controls,
- consent manager records,
- audit / evidence / enforcement workflows,
- and staged implementation aligned to the Rules’ commencement schedule.

> Note: This pack is a product and implementation document for a capstone web app. It is not legal advice.
# Project Abstract

DPDPOS is an enterprise web application that helps organizations build, validate, and operate a compliance framework aligned with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection law. The platform converts statutory obligations into practical software modules: organization onboarding, data inventory, framework generation, notice and consent records, Data Principal request handling, validation rules, violation tracking, remediation workflows, evidence storage, and reporting.

The product is designed around the DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, with attention to the staged commencement of the Rules and the operational responsibilities they place on Data Fiduciaries, Consent Managers, and Significant Data Fiduciaries. The application is not just a checklist or static dashboard; it is an operating system for privacy governance. It gives legal, compliance, and security teams a single workspace to see what personal data exists, why it is processed, whether controls are passing, where violations exist, what remediation is in progress, and what evidence supports each claim.

The system also includes AI-assisted support for summarization, drafting, and explanation, but final compliance decisions remain deterministic and reviewable. By combining rule-based validation, audit-ready evidence, and workflow-driven enforcement, DPDPOS is intended to be a credible capstone project and a commercially relevant product concept for organizations that need structured DPDP compliance management.
